# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or personal identity.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and considerate in communication.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Disruptive or hostile comments.

## Our Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove or edit comments, commits, code, or other contributions that violate these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces (such as GitHub Issues, Discussions, and PRs) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement
If you observe or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainers or via the project’s contact email.

All complaints will be reviewed and handled fairly and promptly.
